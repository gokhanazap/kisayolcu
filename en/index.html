    FAQ

    Things you might be wondering

    Why a website and not an app?

    Because we want you to be able to send a link and say “try this.” No download, no install, no permissions. A tiny Mac app may show up later for the few shortcuts a browser can’t see.

    How does the browser know I pressed ⌘Space?

    It doesn’t 🙂 macOS grabs shortcuts like Spotlight and ⌘Tab before the browser ever sees them. So we watch for the result instead: the page lost focus and came back, an emoji landed in the box, the window changed size… For the ones we truly can’t see (like screenshots), there’s an honest “I did it” button.

    Does it work with non-US keyboards?

    Yes. Most shortcuts are letter-based and work the same everywhere. Symbol keys like @ or [ ] may sit somewhere else on your keyboard, and the on-screen keyboard shows the US layout.

    Does it cost anything?

    Nope, completely free. No account, no ads. If you like it, send it to a friend. That’s plenty.

    Do you collect my data?

    No. There’s no account; your progress and best speedrun time are stored only in this browser.

    Missing a shortcut? Found a bug?

    Drop me a line and I’ll add it. Ideas, suggestions, collabs… I’m all ears.
